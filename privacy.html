Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

Keystone Lead Speed is committed to protecting the privacy of personal information collected through our platform and integrated web forms. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information voluntarily submitted by users through contractor web forms integrated with our platform, including:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Project or service request details

We may also collect registration and contact information from participating contractors using our services.

2. How Information Is Used

Information collected through our platform is used solely to:

  • Facilitate contractor lead notifications
  • Deliver automated call and SMS alerts
  • Improve lead response efficiency
  • Provide service-related communications
  • Maintain system security and reliability

3. Mobile Data and SMS Privacy

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent data, and related messaging information will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties, affiliates, or marketing partners for promotional purposes.

SMS and call data is used exclusively for operational communications directly related to contractor lead response services.

4. Consent to Communications

By submitting a form through a website integrated with Keystone Lead Speed, users consent to receive communications related to their inquiry, including automated text messages and telephone calls where applicable.

5. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on user activity, contractor workflows, and submitted service requests.

6. Opt-Out Instructions

Users may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message received. For assistance, reply HELP.

7. Standard Messaging Disclosures

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your wireless carrier and mobile service plan.

8. Data Retention

Keystone Lead Speed may temporarily retain limited lead information for operational reporting, analytics, fraud prevention, and service improvement purposes. Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate business operations.

9. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at keystoneleadspeed@gmail.com
