Who We Are

Built by a Builder.

Keystone Lead Speed was built by a developer from Pennsylvania who got tired of watching good contractors lose jobs for the dumbest reason — being 10 minutes late to check their email.

This business started with a simple observation. The best roofers, plumbers, and contractors in Pennsylvania aren't losing jobs because they do bad work. They're losing them because they're on the job when the next lead comes in.

A homeowner fills out a form, waits an hour, doesn't hear back, and calls the next guy. The first contractor never even knew the opportunity existed until it was already gone. That's not a skill problem. That's a speed problem.

I built Keystone Lead Speed to solve exactly that. When someone submits your estimate form, your phone rings within seconds. The customer is already on the line. You press one button and you're talking to them — before they've had a chance to call anyone else.

No dashboards to log into. No notifications to swipe. No software to learn. Just your phone, doing what it already does, connected to the right person at the right moment.

This isn't a massive corporation with a sales team. It's one person who built something real, tested it until it worked, and is putting it in the hands of local contractors who deserve a fair shot at every lead they earn.

I set everything up. I provide the support. I answer when you call. That's the business.
