Common Questions

Straight Answers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. We handle the entire setup on our end. You don't touch your website, install software, or learn a new platform. We get you connected and test it before going live.

What if I miss the call?

You'll receive an SMS immediately with the homeowner's full contact information. One tap calls them back. You're still faster than 99% of your competition.

Does it work with my website?

Yes. We integrate with WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, and custom-built sites. If your site has a contact or estimate form, we can connect to it.

Is there a contract?

No contracts. Month to month. Cancel any time. We'd rather keep you because the service works than lock you into something you don't love.

What does the free trial include?

Your first month is completely free with no setup charge. Full Core service, fully operational. We want you to see results before you spend a dollar.

How fast is the response really?

Under 30 seconds in all tested conditions. In most cases it's significantly faster — your phone rings before the homeowner's form confirmation email has even sent.
